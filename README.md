# 🏃 Endless Runner

A fast-paced 2D endless runner where you climb buildings, avoid deadly spikes, and try to achieve the highest score possible.

## 🎮 How to Play

Your goal is to **climb as high as possible while avoiding obstacles**.

* 🏃 Run through the buildings and keep moving forward.
* 🦘 **Jump** over obstacles and gaps.
* 🪂 **Double jump** to reach higher platforms or avoid dangerous situations.
* 🧎 **Slide** under obstacles.
* 🔺 Avoid spikes — hitting them ends your run.
* 🏆 Keep climbing to increase your score and beat your high score!

### Controls

| Key     | Action             |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| `Space` | Jump / Double Jump |
| `Shift`  | Slide              |

**Simple controls. Endless challenge. How high can you climb?**

## ✨ Features

* 🏢 Endless building-climbing gameplay
* 🦘 Double-jump mechanic

* 🔺 Spike hazards
